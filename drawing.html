Sketching my imagination into reality.

Capturing Life Through Pure Observation

My drawing method is rooted in mindful observation—I don’t look at the page while sketching but instead focus entirely on the object itself. This approach allows me to fully immerse myself in the moment, capturing its essence without distraction. Through this technique, I aim to preserve every memorable moment in my life, turning them into raw, honest, and deeply personal works of art.
