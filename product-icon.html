Overview

About project and Objective

About Project:

Vandar, a B2B platform for payments and money management, underwent a complete redesign after three years.

A refreshed logo and custom icon set aligned with the brand’s modern aesthetic, enhancing clarity, usability, and memorability across the platform and marketing materials.

Objective:
  • Created a custom icon set aligned with the platform’s branding and functionality.
  • Ensured consistency in design to enhance clarity and reduce cognitive load.
  • Designed icons to be versatile for use in both the platform and marketing materials.
  • Focused on making the icon set memorable and visually impactful.

My idea was to think outside the box and avoid using images and elements similar to bank cards and money, instead opting for more creative concepts.

Principle

Icons serve as visual representations of our tools and services, making it crucial for them to align with our visual identity and maintain consistency. To achieve this, we use specific shapes in our icons. The chosen shape is a square with varying curves on each side, which helps create a distinctive and cohesive design that reinforces our brand’s identity.
