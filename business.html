Overview

About project and Objective

About Project:

Vandar is a B2B financial platform that simplifies payments, payouts, and money management for businesses.

A key feature of the platform is the business registration process within the Vandar dashboard, where users can create a business account and input essential details to access its tools and services.

Objective:
  • Design a user-friendly and intuitive "Create Business" process to enhance the user experience.
  • Address user pain points, as 20% of all reported issues arise during this stage.
  • Reduce support dependency by streamlining the process, minimizing the need for chat assistance.
  • Ensure clarity and usability to make onboarding smooth and efficient for all users.

Empathy

User observation

I observed 20 users navigating the registration flow and identified an issue with the 'Terms and Conditions' section. Users, influenced by their experiences with other apps, expected a checkbox to approve this section but couldn’t find one.

This mismatch in expectations caused confusion and increased task completion time within the flow."

Empathy

Competitor analyzes

Stripe
Advantages

Seamless Integration: Stripe provides a developer-friendly platform that integrates easily with various e-commerce systems and offers extensive documentation and support.

Disadvantages

Transaction Fees: Stripe charges fees for each transaction, which can impact the overall cost for businesses, especially for high-volume or small transactions.

PayPal Business account
Advantages

Buyer Protection: PayPal provides buyer protection features, which can help build trust and confidence in customers when making transactions.

Disadvantages

Limited Customization: Compared to other payment providers, PayPal offers limited customization options for the payment experience, making it challenging for businesses to maintain a consistent brand image.

Klarna
Advantages

Smooth Checkout Experience: Klarna provides a streamlined and user-friendly checkout process, reducing cart abandonment rates and improving the overall customer experience.

Disadvantages

Limited Availability: Klarna may not be available in all regions or for all types of businesses, limiting its accessibility for some merchants and customers.

Empathy

Heuristic evaluation

As a product designer, I conducted a heuristic evaluation to identify usability issues impacting the user experience. Using a set of heuristics, I uncovered several problems, including confusing navigation, unclear instructions, and inconsistent design elements.

The findings guided improvements to the login and registration processes, ensuring the design became more intuitive, efficient, and user-friendly.

Define

User Flow

After analyzing the identified issues, we concluded that the account creation process needs improvements across multiple layers.

Define

Categorized issues

UX Improvements:
  • Enhance the user flow by providing clear progress indicators and showing users their current position, helping them understand the next steps.
  • Refine and update components within the design system for consistency and usability.
  • Improve UX writing to provide clearer, more helpful instructions and feedback.
UI Improvements:
  • Redesign the entire account creation interface to give it a more unique, modern, and visually appealing look.
Problem statement

Users navigating the system struggle due to a lack of clear progress indicators and an understanding of their current position within the flow. This absence creates uncertainty about the next steps, resulting in confusion and a less seamless user experience.
